Child Safety Standards

Special Sathi

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Special Sathi is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment for all users. We have a zero-tolerance approach toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Any use of Special Sathi for the sexual exploitation or abuse of children is strictly prohibited.

Zero-Tolerance Policy: Special Sathi does not permit content, behavior, communication, solicitation, grooming, exploitation, or other activity involving child sexual abuse or exploitation.

2. Prohibited Child-Safety Content and Behavior

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Special Sathi:

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Creation, upload, sharing, possession, or distribution of CSAM.
  • Sexual solicitation or exploitation of minors.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish inappropriate relationships with minors.
  • Sexualized communication involving minors.
  • Using the platform to facilitate the sexual exploitation of children.
  • Requests for, offers of, or exchanges involving CSAM.
  • Any other behavior that violates applicable child-safety laws.

Accounts and content associated with prohibited activity may be restricted, suspended, or permanently removed, subject to our applicable policies and procedures.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report child-safety concerns through the reporting functionality available within the Special Sathi application.

How to Report

  • Open the relevant user profile or conversation.
  • Select the available reporting option.
  • Choose the appropriate reason for the report.
  • Provide relevant details and submit the report.

Users should provide sufficient information to help our team understand and investigate the reported concern.

Safety concerns may also be reported to our designated contact address:

4. Review and Action on Reports

Special Sathi reviews reports and takes appropriate action based on the nature and circumstances of the reported activity.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

  • Reviewing reported profiles, messages, or other content.
  • Removing content that violates our policies.
  • Restricting or suspending user accounts.
  • Permanently terminating accounts where appropriate.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally permitted or required.
  • Escalating matters to appropriate authorities where required by law.

5. Handling of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Special Sathi prohibits CSAM and other forms of child sexual exploitation content.

When suspected CSAM or child sexual exploitation is identified, Special Sathi may take appropriate action in accordance with applicable laws, platform requirements, and our internal safety procedures.

Where legally required, information may be reported to appropriate regional or national authorities and relevant law-enforcement organizations.

6. Cooperation With Authorities

Special Sathi may cooperate with law-enforcement agencies and other competent authorities regarding suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation when required or permitted by applicable law.

We may provide information or preserve relevant records when legally required and subject to applicable privacy and legal requirements.

7. User Safety Responsibilities

Users are expected to use Special Sathi responsibly and report suspicious or unsafe behavior.

Users must not use the platform to contact, solicit, exploit, groom, or otherwise engage in prohibited activity involving minors.

If you believe a child may be at immediate risk of harm, contact the appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement authorities.

We may update our safety practices and procedures when necessary to reflect changes in applicable laws, regulatory requirements, or platform safety standards.
