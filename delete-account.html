Account & Data Deletion

Special Sathi respects your privacy and provides users with the ability to delete their account and associated personal data.

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Special Sathi account directly from the mobile application.

Steps to request account deletion:

  1. Open the Special Sathi app.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the information displayed on the confirmation screen.
  6. Confirm that you want to delete your account.

7-Day Account Restoration Period

When you request account deletion, your account enters a 7-day deletion period.

During this period, your account is pending permanent deletion. You have the opportunity to restore your account if you change your mind.

1

Deletion Requested

Your account is marked for deletion after you confirm the delete account request.

2

Within 7 Days

You can sign in and use the available Restore Account option to cancel the pending deletion and reactivate your account.

3

After 7 Days

If you do not restore your account within the 7-day period, your account will be permanently deleted.

Important: After the 7-day restoration period has ended and permanent deletion has been completed, the account cannot be restored through the application's account restoration process.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After the 7-day deletion period, when permanent deletion is completed, the following account-associated personal data will be deleted, subject to applicable legal, security and regulatory requirements:

  • Mobile number and account information
  • Name and basic profile information
  • Date of birth, gender, education and occupation information
  • Matrimony and profile details
  • Profile photographs
  • Location information associated with your account
  • Uploaded identity and disability verification documents
  • Account-related preferences and settings
  • Other personal information associated with your account
Permanent Deletion: Once the 7-day restoration period has ended, the account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted, except information that is required to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where necessary for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Legal or regulatory compliance
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Resolving disputes
  • Enforcing our terms and policies
  • Maintaining necessary technical or security records

Any retained information will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

Account Deactivation

If you do not want to permanently delete your account, Special Sathi also provides an Account Deactivation option.

Deactivation is different from account deletion. A deactivated account is temporarily disabled and may be reactivated according to the available reactivation process.

Deactivation does not start the 7-day permanent account deletion process unless you separately choose the Delete Account option.

Unable to Access the App?

If you cannot access your Special Sathi account and need to request account deletion, please contact the Special Sathi support team through the contact or support information provided by Special Sathi.

You may be asked to provide sufficient information to verify account ownership before a deletion request can be processed.

Security Notice: Never send your password, OTP, or other authentication credentials when requesting account deletion.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Special Sathi collects, uses, stores and protects personal information, please refer to the Special Sathi Privacy Policy.

Important Information

Account deletion is a permanent action after the 7-day restoration period has ended.

If you restore your account during the 7-day period, the pending deletion request will be cancelled and your account can continue to be used according to the application's available features.

After permanent deletion has been completed, the account cannot be recovered through the account restoration process.
