Privacy Policy Effective date: 16 October 2026 Last updated: 16 October 2026 1. Introduction Clientel (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides a software platform that helps clinics, hospitals, and other healthcare providers manage appointment-related communications through the WhatsApp Business Platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you use our website, software, and related services. 2. Information We Collect Depending on how you use our services, we may collect the following information: * Business account information: Clinic or hospital name, business contact details, and account configuration. * User information: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, login details, and information about authorized staff using our platform. * WhatsApp integration information: WhatsApp Business Account identifiers, phone number identifiers, business profile details, authorization tokens, and integration status. * Appointment and notification information: Patient names, phone numbers, appointment dates and times, reminder status, and notification delivery records, where provided by the healthcare provider. * Technical information: IP addresses, browser information, device details, system logs, and diagnostic data. * Support communications: Information you provide when contacting us for assistance. We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to provide and maintain our services. 3. How We Use Information We may use information to: * Provide and operate our software platform. * Connect customer business accounts to the WhatsApp Business Platform. * Send appointment reminders and other notifications requested by authorized healthcare providers. * Track message submission, delivery, failure, and other available status events. * Maintain account security and prevent misuse. * Troubleshoot technical issues and improve our services. * Respond to customer support requests. * Comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not use patient information for our own advertising purposes. 4. Healthcare Provider Responsibilities Clinics, hospitals, and other healthcare providers using our platform are responsible for determining the lawful basis for processing patient information, obtaining any required permissions or consent, and ensuring that their communications comply with applicable laws and WhatsApp policies. Healthcare providers should avoid sending sensitive medical information through notifications unless they have determined that doing so is appropriate and legally permitted. Our platform is intended to support healthcare providers' communication workflows. Unless separately agreed in writing, we do not provide medical care or act as a healthcare provider. 5. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services Our platform may integrate with the WhatsApp Business Platform and other third-party service providers. When a healthcare provider connects its WhatsApp Business Account, information may be processed by Meta and WhatsApp in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy policies. We may also use third-party providers for hosting, database services, security, monitoring, and technical support. These providers may process information on our behalf under appropriate contractual or other safeguards. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party services. 6. How We Share Information We may share information: * With the clinic, hospital, or business that uses our platform. * With Meta and WhatsApp as needed to provide messaging functionality. * With service providers that help us operate and secure our platform. * When required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid government request. * To investigate fraud, protect our rights, or help protect the safety and security of our users. We do not sell personal information to third parties. 7.
