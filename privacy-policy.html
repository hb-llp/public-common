Special Sathi - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Special Sathi ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Special Sathi mobile application.

Important: Special Sathi is a matrimony application designed to help users create profiles and connect with potential partners.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect the following information:

  • Mobile phone number used for OTP-based authentication.
  • Name and basic profile information.
  • Date of birth, gender and other profile information provided by you.
  • Education, occupation and other matrimony-related information.
  • Profile photographs.
  • Identity and supporting documents submitted for verification.
  • Location information when required for application functionality.
  • Device and technical information required for application operation.
  • Information related to your communication and use of application features.

2. Camera and Photo Gallery

Special Sathi allows users to add profile photographs and other required images using either the device camera or the device photo gallery.

The camera may be used when you choose to capture a new photograph directly from within the application.

Photo and media access may be used when you choose to select an existing photograph from your device gallery.

Camera and photo access are requested only when the related feature is used. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

3. Documents and Verification

Special Sathi may allow users to submit documents for identity or eligibility verification.

Documents submitted through the application may include identity or disability-related verification documents where required by the application.

These documents are used for verification and related application purposes and are handled according to applicable security and privacy practices.

4. Location Information

Special Sathi may request access to your device location when required for specific application functionality.

Location permission is requested through the device permission system. You may manage or revoke location permission through your device settings.

5. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your Special Sathi account.
  • To authenticate your account using OTP.
  • To create and display your matrimony profile.
  • To provide profile matching and related matrimony services.
  • To verify submitted information or documents.
  • To enable communication and chat features.
  • To send important notifications and service-related messages.
  • To improve application functionality and user experience.
  • To prevent misuse, fraud, unauthorized access and security issues.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

6. Profile Information and Visibility

Information that you choose to add to your matrimony profile may be visible to other users according to the application's profile visibility and privacy settings.

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you choose to publish on your profile is accurate and appropriate.

7. Chat and Communication

Special Sathi may provide communication features such as chat between users. Information exchanged through these features may be processed to provide and maintain the communication service.

8. Notifications

The application may send notifications related to account activity, profile activity, messages, verification, and other important service updates.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

9. Languages

Special Sathi provides application content in multiple languages to improve accessibility and usability.

The application currently supports:

Changing the application language does not change how your personal information is collected or processed.

11. Data Sharing

12. Account Deletion and Deactivation

Special Sathi may provide options to deactivate or delete your account through the application.
